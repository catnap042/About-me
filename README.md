# Hi, I'm catnap42 👋

**Battery R&D Engineer | Electrochemical Modeling | Scientific Computing | AI for Engineering**

I work at the intersection of **battery science, physics-based modeling, and data-driven engineering**. With a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering and experience spanning academic research and industrial R&D, I focus on turning complex electrochemical behavior into interpretable models, reliable analyses, and reproducible software workflows.

My interests extend beyond batteries to scientific computing, applied AI, and tools that make engineering research more efficient and transparent.

## 🔬 What I Work On

- **Battery diagnostics:** open-circuit voltage (OCV), incremental-capacity analysis (dQ/dV), differential-voltage analysis (dV/dQ), and degradation interpretation.
- **Modeling and simulation:** equivalent-circuit models (ECM), transmission-line models (TLM), parameter estimation, and physics-informed approaches.
- **Battery health and lifetime:** state-of-health assessment, aging analysis, and prediction methods with attention to validation and uncertainty.
- **Scientific computing:** Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib, and Jupyter-based research workflows.
- **AI for engineering:** practical AI-assisted methods for coding, data analysis, technical communication, and research automation.
